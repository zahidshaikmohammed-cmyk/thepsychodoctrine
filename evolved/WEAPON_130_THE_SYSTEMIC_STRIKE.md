# WEAPON 130 — THE SYSTEMIC STRIKE

One good call is not a system; repeatable quality is.
Build a sequence: understand, explain, verify, invite, follow up—with permission.
Record what the prospect asked and what you promised.
Keep claims consistent across calls and channels.
Review outcomes to find where people become confused or disengage.
Fix the process instead of blaming the prospect.
A system should make honest selling easier to repeat.
Psycho is the standard behind every step.
