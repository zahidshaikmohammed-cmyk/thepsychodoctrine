# Weapon 119 — Decision Gravity Well

A decision becomes clearer as the important facts come into focus.

Psycho gathers the need, the evidence, the cost, and the trade-offs into one concise summary.

Say: “With all of that in view, what question remains unresolved?”

Keep attention on the real decision, not the seller’s desire to close.

Relevance brings the conversation together. The facts should do the work.
