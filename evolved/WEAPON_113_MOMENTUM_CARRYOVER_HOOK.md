# Weapon 113 — Momentum Carryover Hook

Good conversations create useful momentum when the next step is specific.

Before ending, recap what was learned, what remains unresolved, and who will do what next.

Say: “Let’s capture the next step so the useful work continues.”

A date, an owner, and a clear purpose are stronger than vague enthusiasm.

Psycho carries momentum forward through follow-through—not through pressure.
