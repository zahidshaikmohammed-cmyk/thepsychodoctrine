# WEAPON 152 — THE PRESSURE PARADOX

Pressure can focus attention—or make people stop thinking.
Use direct questions, not intimidation.
Ask: “Would a clear next step help, or do you need more time?”
Match the pace to the prospect’s stated urgency.
Never disguise your own quota as their emergency.
The right amount of pressure is enough to clarify, never enough to suffocate.
If the conversation becomes tense, lower the temperature.
Precision beats force.
