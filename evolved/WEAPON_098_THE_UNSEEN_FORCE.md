# Weapon 098 — The Unseen Force

The unseen force behind a strong decision is often alignment: the offer fits the need, the terms are understood, and the risks are acceptable.

Psycho makes that alignment testable.

Say: “Can you connect this option to the outcome you want—and name the main trade-off?”

If the connection is weak, do not cover it with energy. Reassess the fit.

When the reason is real, the decision does not need a hidden push. It can stand in daylight.
