# Weapon 114 — Now-or-Never Neutralizer

“Now or never” is rarely a useful description of a complex decision. It turns a choice into a threat.

Psycho replaces the false binary with the real options: proceed, wait for specific information, choose another path, or decline.

Say: “These are the options as they stand. Which one fits your situation best?”

If a genuine deadline changes those options, explain it plainly.

Clarity gives a decision strength. A manufactured ultimatum only gives it noise.
