# Weapon 080 — The Final Judgement

Every offer reaches a moment of judgment: does the value justify the cost, the risk, and the commitment?

Psycho does not deliver the verdict for the client. He puts the evidence on the table and lets it stand.

Say: “Based on what we’ve covered, does this fit—or should we leave it here?”

A clear yes moves forward. A clear no closes the loop. An unresolved question gets an honest answer.

The final judgment is not domination. It is a decision made with open eyes.
