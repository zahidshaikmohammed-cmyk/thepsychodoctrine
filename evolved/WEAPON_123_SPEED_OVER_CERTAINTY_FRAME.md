# Weapon 123 — Speed Over Certainty Frame

Speed matters, but a fast decision with missing information can become an expensive mistake.

Psycho checks what must be known before moving and what can be learned through a small test.

Say: “What should we verify before taking the next step?”

Move quickly when the stakes are low and the path is reversible. Take more care when the consequences are lasting.

Good pace matches the decision—not the seller’s impatience.
