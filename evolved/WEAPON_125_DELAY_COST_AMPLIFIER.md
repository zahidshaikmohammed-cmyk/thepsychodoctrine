# Weapon 125 — Delay Cost Amplifier

The cost of delay should be measured, not dramatized.

Psycho identifies what waiting could affect: time, budget, access, effort, or the chance to solve the original problem. He marks uncertainty honestly and avoids turning possibilities into guarantees.

Say: “Which cost of waiting matters most to you, and how can we estimate it fairly?”

If the cost is small, say so. If it is significant and supported by facts, show the evidence.

A clear estimate helps a client choose. An exaggerated warning only weakens trust.
