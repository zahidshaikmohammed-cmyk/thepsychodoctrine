# WEAPON 150 — THE FINAL ROUND TABLE

Bring the decision back to the prospect’s own priorities.
Summarize the goal, the evidence, the cost, and the remaining uncertainty.
Ask which option best fits their circumstances.
Make room for action, delay, or a respectful no.
Do not crown yourself the authority over their future.
A strong close is a clear agreement between informed adults.
Confirm the chosen next step and who owns it.
Close with dignity. Leave trust intact.
