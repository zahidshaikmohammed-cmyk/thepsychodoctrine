# Weapon 091 — The Guilt of Inaction

Guilt is a poor sales tool. It makes people defend themselves instead of evaluating the offer.

Psycho replaces blame with consequence. Waiting may preserve flexibility; it may also delay a useful solution. Both possibilities deserve honest examination.

Say: “What would waiting help you confirm, and what might it cost?”

No attack on character. No claim that caution proves weakness.

The goal is not to make the client feel guilty for doing nothing. It is to help them understand the real cost of each path.
