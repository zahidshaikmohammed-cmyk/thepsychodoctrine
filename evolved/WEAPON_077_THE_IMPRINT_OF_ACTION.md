# Weapon 077 — The Imprint of Action

Every action leaves an imprint. A missed promise leaves one. A clear answer leaves one. A difficult truth delivered without theatre leaves the deepest.

Psycho does not try to carve a decision into someone else’s mind. He makes the quality of his own work impossible to forget.

Say: “Here is what happens next, what it costs, and what you can expect.”

Then deliver exactly that.

The call ends. The impression remains. Let it be proof of discipline, not pressure.
