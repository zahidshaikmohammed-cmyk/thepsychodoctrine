# Weapon 112 — Pause Intolerance Spike

Silence is not a problem that must be defeated. It can mean thought, uncertainty, or a need for space.

Psycho stays calm and lets the pause do its work. He does not fill every second with another argument.

Say: “Take a moment. What question is still on your mind?”

Listen to the answer without interruption.

Composure is not the power to make another person uncomfortable. It is the discipline to remain clear when the room goes quiet.
