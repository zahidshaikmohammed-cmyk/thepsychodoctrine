# WEAPON 131 — THE PAYMENT RITUAL

Payment is a transaction, not a transformation spell.
Before asking for it, confirm the product, total cost, terms, risks, and cancellation rules.
Ask the buyer to explain what they believe they are purchasing.
Resolve any mismatch before proceeding.
Never imply that paying guarantees success or a new identity.
Make the next steps and receipt clear.
A sound decision deserves a transparent process.
The ritual is accountability on both sides.
