# WEAPON 006 — THE ENTRY TRIGGER

*The entry trigger is the crack that lets your fire in.*

Attention is not agreement.

A person can hear every word and still be somewhere else. Their mind is on the next meeting, the unfinished task, the last seller who wasted their time.

You can keep talking.

They can keep not listening.

**A MESSAGE THAT NEVER LANDS IS JUST NOISE WITH A PHONE BILL.**

The Entry Trigger is the first detail that makes the conversation relevant enough to examine.

Not a trick. Not a fake secret. Not a hint that you know something the market has not heard.

A real detail.

A company. A change. A question. A fact that connects to the reason you called.

“There's been a change worth examining in [company]. I want to show you what changed, what the numbers actually say, and what could still go wrong.”

Now there is a subject.

Now there is something to test.

Now the listener can decide whether it deserves another minute.

**CURIOSITY OPENS THE DOOR. TRUTH HAS TO WALK THROUGH IT.**

Don't throw out a stock name like it is a password to someone's greed. Don't wave the word *pre-market* around like a badge of secret access. If the information is public, say what it is. If you have not verified it, do not present it as fact.

A trigger without substance is bait.

And bait has a short life.

The strongest opening detail is often small: a change in guidance, a price move with a clear cause, a risk the market may be underestimating, or a question that reveals whether the subject matters to this person at all.

Specific beats spectacular.

A clean fact beats a dramatic hint.

**DON'T MAKE THEM FEEL THEY MISSED SOMETHING. SHOW THEM WHAT IS WORTH LOOKING AT.**

Then stop.

Give the detail room to work. Let them ask. Let them challenge it. Let them say it is irrelevant.

The trigger has done its job when attention becomes a real conversation—not when the other person is too unsettled to think.

You don't control the map because someone listened.

You earn the next step by proving you know where you are going.

**NO BAIT. NO BLUFF. ONE REAL DETAIL THAT CUTS THROUGH THE NOISE.**

That is the Entry Trigger.
