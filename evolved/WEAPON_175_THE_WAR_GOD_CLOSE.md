# WEAPON 175 — THE WAR GOD CLOSE

Bring conviction to the close without pretending to be more than a salesperson.
Summarize the prospect’s goal, the offer’s fit, its costs, and its limitations.
Ask: “Does this make sense for you, or is there something we still need to resolve?”
Answer plainly and respect the final choice.
Confidence does not require intimidation or a theatrical persona.
The strongest close combines energy with accountability.
Be memorable for the quality of the conversation.
Finish with clarity, not conquest.
