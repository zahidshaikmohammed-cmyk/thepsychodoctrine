# WEAPON 072 — THE ART OF PERSISTENCE

PERSISTENCE IS RETURNING WITH A REASON.

A weak seller repeats the same pitch until the words lose their teeth. Psycho returns only when there is something useful to bring: a missing answer, a relevant update, a clearer comparison, a better-fit option.

“Last time, this was the point that needed clarity. Has anything changed—or should I close the loop here?”

That is persistence with a spine.

Follow up when promised. Keep the message brief. Bring value, not another wave of noise. If the prospect says no or asks for no further contact, respect it immediately. Discipline includes knowing when the mission is over.

Psycho does not wear down a person’s boundaries. He wears down confusion by doing the work the last conversation revealed.

The objective is not to become impossible to escape. It is to become worth hearing again.

**RETURN WITH VALUE. RESPECT THE LINE. LET THE WORK SPEAK.**
