# WEAPON 164 — THE INNER ROOM

Invite reflection without asking someone to reveal more than they want.
Ask: “What is the main concern you’re weighing up?”
Let them decide how much to share.
Summarize the issue and offer relevant information or options.
Never turn a sales call into forced emotional excavation.
Private thoughts remain theirs.
A useful conversation opens a door; it does not kick one down.
Respect is part of the method.
Clarity does not require intrusion.
