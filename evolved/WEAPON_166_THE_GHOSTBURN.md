# WEAPON 166 — THE GHOSTBURN

If a prospect stops replying, send one brief, respectful follow-up if contact is appropriate.
Refer to the actual conversation and offer a simple way to respond.
Do not imply that silence means they fear change or owe you an answer.
If they do not reply, stop contacting them unless they have invited further follow-up.
A professional follow-up makes re-entry easy and refusal safe.
Never turn persistence into haunting.
Respect is memorable too.
Leave the door open without standing in it.
