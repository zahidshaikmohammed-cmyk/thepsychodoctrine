# WEAPON 132 — THE SHADOW TRACKER

Listen for the concern beneath a vague objection—but do not assume you know it.
Ask: “When you say you’re not ready, what specifically is missing?”
It may be budget, timing, trust, information, or simply no interest.
Reflect the answer back and check that you understood.
Offer relevant facts, not a diagnosis of their character.
If the gap cannot be solved, say so plainly.
Clarity tracks the concern without attacking the person.
That is the useful shadow to follow.
