# Weapon 082 — The Finality of Action

Action turns intention into reality. It also makes the details matter.

Before the final step, confirm the product, price, terms, risks, and next actions. Never treat a click or signature as a substitute for understanding.

Say: “Would you like to proceed on these exact terms?”

If the answer is yes, execute cleanly. If it is no, stop cleanly.

Psycho respects the weight of action. A close is not a victory over the client; it is the beginning of the obligation to deliver.
