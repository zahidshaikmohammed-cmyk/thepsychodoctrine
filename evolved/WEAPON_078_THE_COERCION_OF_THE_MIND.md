# Weapon 078 — The Coercion of the Mind

A mind under pressure does not become wiser. It becomes defensive.

Psycho refuses to confuse resistance with weakness or hesitation with permission to push harder. He finds the real question beneath the objection.

Say: “What part still doesn’t feel clear?”

Then listen without preparing a counterattack.

Clarity can change a decision. Coercion can only contaminate it.

The sharpest mind in the room is the one that can stop talking long enough to understand.
