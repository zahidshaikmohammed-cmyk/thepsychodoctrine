# Weapon 089 — The Final Turning Point

A turning point is not created by a dramatic sentence. It appears when the evidence meets a genuine need.

Psycho identifies that moment without exaggerating it. He summarizes the problem, the proposed answer, and the trade-offs.

Say: “We started with this problem. Here is what this option can solve—and what it cannot.”

Then ask whether the fit is strong enough to proceed.

The turning point belongs to the client. Psycho’s craft is making it clear enough to recognize.
