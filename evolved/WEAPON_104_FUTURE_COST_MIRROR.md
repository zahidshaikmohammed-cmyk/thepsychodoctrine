# Weapon 104 — Future Cost Mirror

Every option has a cost. Some costs appear immediately; others become visible only with time.

Psycho helps the client compare these possibilities without exaggeration.

Ask: “If nothing changes over the next month, what practical effect would that have?”

Use real facts, known numbers, and the client’s own priorities. Do not invent losses or predict disaster.

The mirror is for clear comparison—not fear. A sound decision begins when the trade-offs of each path can be seen.
