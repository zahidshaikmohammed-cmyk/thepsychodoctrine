# WEAPON 060 — THE INEVITABLE MOVE

The decision is not just about what they’re buying.
It’s about them taking action that can never be undone.
Psycho doesn’t ask them to think.
He makes them act — and in that act, their future is set.
The decision sets them in motion.
Psycho forces their hand and they move with inevitability.
The future begins with action.
Once they decide, the shift is made.

## PSYCHO EVOLUTION — TURN AGREEMENT INTO ACTION

A DECISION WITHOUT A NEXT STEP IS JUST A SENTENCE IN THE AIR.

Once the client has chosen freely, Psycho turns the agreement into a concrete sequence. Who does what? What happens first? What is the deadline? What confirmation or preparation is needed?

**Say:** “Good. The next step is this. I’ll handle my part by this date, and you’ll receive the details in writing. Does that work?”

Make the action small enough to begin and clear enough to verify. Send the summary. Confirm the scope. Set the kickoff. Track the commitment. If a dependency changes, communicate it before it becomes a surprise.

Movement is not the same as rushing. A client should understand the commitment before taking it—and should not be told that a decision can never be reconsidered when the agreement says otherwise.

Psycho makes momentum real through execution. No theatrical countdown. No forced hand. Just a clear decision followed by disciplined action.

**THE RULE:** Confirm the choice. Define the next step. Execute what was promised.
