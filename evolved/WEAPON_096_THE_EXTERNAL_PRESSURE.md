# Weapon 096 — The External Pressure

External pressure is real: budgets, deadlines, market changes, competing priorities. It must be described accurately, not amplified for effect.

Psycho names the constraint and shows its actual consequence.

Say: “This is the deadline we can verify. Here is what changes after it—and what does not.”

If there is no deadline, say so. If the facts are uncertain, disclose that too.

Pressure loses its power to deceive when the truth is placed beside it. Psycho would rather lose a sale than invent a reason to rush one.
