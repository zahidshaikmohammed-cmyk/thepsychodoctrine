# WEAPON 157 — THE PAYMENT DEATHLINE

Payment should mark a clear transaction, not a symbolic death of doubt.
Before taking payment, confirm the price, terms, risks, and refund or cancellation policy.
Ask whether anything remains unclear.
Never make a card swipe sound like proof of courage or commitment to a new identity.
The buyer should know exactly what happens next.
Transparent payment protects both sides.
No emotional theatre can replace informed consent.
Let the transaction be clean.
