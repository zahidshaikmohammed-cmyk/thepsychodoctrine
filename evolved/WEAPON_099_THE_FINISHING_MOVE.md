# Weapon 099 — The Finishing Move

The finishing move is precision.

Summarize the client’s goal, the solution, the full cost, the important risks, and the next step. Then ask for a direct answer.

Say: “Does this meet your requirements, and would you like to proceed?”

Do not smother the answer with another pitch. Do not treat silence as consent.

If yes, confirm the agreement. If no, close respectfully. If uncertain, identify the missing information.

Psycho finishes the conversation cleanly, whatever the outcome.
