# Weapon 109 — Time Scarcity Implant

Time is limited. That truth needs no theatrical countdown.

Psycho distinguishes a real limit from a manufactured one. A genuine deadline gets explained; an open timeline stays open.

Say: “Here is the actual time constraint, and here is what remains flexible.”

The client deserves facts, not a feeling engineered to hurry them.

Time is useful when it helps people prioritize. Scarcity becomes deception when it is invented.
