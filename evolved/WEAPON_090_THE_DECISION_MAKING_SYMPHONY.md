# Weapon 090 — The Decision-Making Symphony

A decision has several instruments: facts, emotion, timing, risk, and trust. Let one drown out the others and the whole arrangement breaks.

Psycho brings them into rhythm. He explains the offer, listens for concerns, answers directly, and pauses when the client needs space.

Say: “Which part of this decision needs the most attention now?”

No single line closes every deal. The skill is knowing what the moment requires.

A clean close sounds less like a command and more like every important question finally finding its answer.
