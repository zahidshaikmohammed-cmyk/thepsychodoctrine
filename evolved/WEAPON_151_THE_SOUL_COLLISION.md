# WEAPON 151 — THE SOUL COLLISION

Bring the conversation back to what the prospect genuinely values.
Ask: “What matters most about solving this problem?”
Listen for their own words instead of supplying a grand meaning for them.
Connect the offer to that priority only when the fit is real.
Do not frame hesitation as betrayal of self.
A powerful conversation can touch identity without exploiting it.
Let values clarify the decision; let facts test the offer.
Collision becomes clarity when both remain honest.
