# WEAPON 129 — THE ABSENCE CLOSE

A conversation can end cleanly without theatrical disappearance.
Summarize what was discussed and what remains unanswered.
Ask whether a follow-up would be useful, and agree on a time if they want one.
If they decline, respect the answer and leave the door open without chasing.
Your absence should demonstrate professionalism, not punishment.
Do not vanish mid-call to make someone anxious.
A clear exit protects trust.
The strongest final impression is respect.
