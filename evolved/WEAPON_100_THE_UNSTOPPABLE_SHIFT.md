# Weapon 100 — The Unstoppable Shift

The unstoppable shift is not a client pushed past their judgment. It is a seller who has outgrown the need to force outcomes.

Psycho prepares harder, listens closer, speaks cleaner, and accepts the truth faster. He knows a bad-fit sale is not a victory.

Say: “The right decision is the one that fits the facts and your priorities.”

When the fit is real, move with conviction. When it is not, step back without fear.

The doctrine reaches its hundredth weapon here: power without deception, intensity without coercion, and a standard that survives the close.
