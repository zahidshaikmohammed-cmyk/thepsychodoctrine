# Weapon 086 — The Will of the Client

The client’s will is not a tool to seize. It is the center of the decision.

Psycho’s role is to help people articulate their own priorities, then test whether the offer serves them.

Say: “Which option best matches what you said you wanted?”

The answer may be yes, no, or not yet. All three deserve respect.

A strong closer does not replace the client’s judgment with his own. He makes sure the client has enough clarity to use that judgment well.
