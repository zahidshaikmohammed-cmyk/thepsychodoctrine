# Weapon 106 — Momentum Ignition Line

Momentum begins with a step small enough to take and meaningful enough to matter.

Psycho identifies the next useful action: a demonstration, a comparison, a trial, or a follow-up with a clear purpose.

Say: “What is one concrete step that would help you evaluate this properly?”

Momentum is not speed for its own sake. It is progress that produces information.

One deliberate move can break a loop that ten more speeches never will.
