# WEAPON 143 — THE NON HUMAN STANDARD

Do not perform a fake persona to seem powerful.
Be prepared, consistent, and unmistakably human.
Know the offer and admit what you do not know.
Use a tone that fits the conversation rather than trying to dominate it.
Respectful confidence beats manufactured mystique.
The prospect should remember the quality of the help, not a theatrical character.
Standards are demonstrated through behaviour.
Let reliability become your signature.
