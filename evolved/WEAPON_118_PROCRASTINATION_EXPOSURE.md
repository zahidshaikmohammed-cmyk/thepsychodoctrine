# Weapon 118 — Procrastination Exposure

Sometimes a decision keeps moving to tomorrow because the next step feels too large or too vague.

Psycho makes the obstacle specific without judging the person.

Ask: “What is the smallest part of this decision we can resolve now?”

Break the work into a question, a check, or a short comparison. If the timing is genuinely wrong, set a realistic point to return.

Expose the pattern, not the person. Small clarity can restore movement.
