# WEAPON 162 — THE UNMARKED GRAVE

Do not use fear of being forgotten as a reason to buy.
Help the prospect define what meaningful progress looks like to them.
Ask what they want to build, learn, or improve.
Then examine whether the offer supports that goal at a reasonable cost.
Do not use legacy or mortality to shame a decision.
Meaning is personal; the seller does not get to assign it.
Make the goal visible and the trade-offs honest.
A life’s value is never measured by a sale.
