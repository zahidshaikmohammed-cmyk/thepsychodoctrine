# Weapon 094 — The Finality of Yes

A yes is the start of a commitment, not permission to stop being careful.

Psycho confirms the exact agreement, records the next steps, and makes sure the client knows how delivery will work.

Say: “Let me confirm what you’ve agreed to, so there are no surprises.”

If the client misunderstood, correct it before proceeding. If a term changes, disclose it.

The finality of yes comes from a clear agreement and reliable follow-through—not from making the client feel unable to reconsider.
