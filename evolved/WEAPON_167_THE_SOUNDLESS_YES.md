# WEAPON 167 — THE SOUNDLESS YES

A real yes is clear, informed, and voluntary—not inferred from silence.
After answering questions, ask directly whether they want to proceed.
Give them enough time to consider the choice.
If their answer is uncertain, clarify what remains unresolved.
Do not treat a long pause as a win.
Confirm the agreed next step in plain language.
Consent should be audible and unambiguous.
A quiet moment is not a contract.
