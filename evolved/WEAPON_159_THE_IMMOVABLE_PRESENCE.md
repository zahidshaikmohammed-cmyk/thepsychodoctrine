# WEAPON 159 — THE IMMOVABLE PRESENCE

Be steady about facts and flexible about the person’s needs.
Know the offer, its limits, and the evidence behind every claim.
When challenged, answer directly instead of turning the call into a contest.
Admit uncertainty when it exists.
A firm standard does not require stubbornness or intimidation.
Trust grows when confidence survives scrutiny.
Stay grounded without demanding that the prospect move.
Immovable integrity; adaptable conversation.
