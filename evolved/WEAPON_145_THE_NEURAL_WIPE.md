# WEAPON 145 — THE NEURAL WIPE

Do not promise to reprogram someone in a sales call.
Help them replace confusion with a repeatable decision process.
List the goal, evidence, cost, risk, and unanswered questions.
Invite them to check the facts independently.
Let new information update their view rather than demanding loyalty to a pitch.
A good conversation improves judgment; it does not erase identity.
Make the process easy to revisit.
Clarity should remain after the call ends.
