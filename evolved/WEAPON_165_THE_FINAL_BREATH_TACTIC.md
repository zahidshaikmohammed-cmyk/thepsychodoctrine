# WEAPON 165 — THE FINAL BREATH TACTIC

Do not invoke mortality to rush a commercial decision.
Instead, ask how important the goal is relative to the prospect’s other priorities.
Clarify the cost, risk, and realistic time horizon.
If the decision is consequential, encourage a thoughtful review.
Urgency should come from genuine circumstances, not imagined last days.
Time matters because it is limited—not because a seller dramatizes it.
Keep perspective without fear theatre.
A decision made calmly can still be decisive.
