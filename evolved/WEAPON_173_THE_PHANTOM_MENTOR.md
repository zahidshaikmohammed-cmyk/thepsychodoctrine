# WEAPON 173 — THE PHANTOM MENTOR

Explain the support, tools, and expertise the offer genuinely includes.
Be clear about what the buyer must do independently and where outside help may be useful.
Do not create dependency or imply that success requires loyalty to one seller.
A mentor makes the path clearer, not narrower.
Invite questions about fit and limitations.
Let the work—and the evidence—speak.
Support should increase independence.
