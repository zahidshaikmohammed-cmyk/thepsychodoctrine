# WEAPON 127 — THE PRICE OF SILENCE

Silence can hide a question that has not yet been answered.
Do not treat every pause as resistance.
Ask: “What part still feels unclear?”
Give the prospect room to think without filling the space.
If waiting changes the facts, explain exactly how—and only when that change is real.
Never invent a countdown to force a response.
The value of a pause is what it reveals.
Psycho turns silence into listening, then clarity.
