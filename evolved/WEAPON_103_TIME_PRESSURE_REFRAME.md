# Weapon 103 — Time Pressure Reframe

Pressure distorts time. A short deadline can make a small decision feel enormous; endless postponement can make an important decision feel weightless.

Psycho restores scale.

Say: “How important is this decision, and what timeline does it genuinely require?”

Separate verified deadlines from imagined urgency. Separate useful reflection from circular delay.

Time pressure should sharpen attention, not erase judgment. The frame is simple: match the pace to the stakes, then move with purpose.
