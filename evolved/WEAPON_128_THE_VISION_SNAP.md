# WEAPON 128 — THE VISION SNAP

Invite the prospect to picture the outcome they actually want.
Ask: “If nothing changed over the next six months, what would that mean to you?”
Listen without supplying a frightening answer for them.
Then ask what a realistic improvement would look like.
Connect the offer only to outcomes it can reasonably support.
A future vision is a compass, not a guarantee.
The snap comes when the goal becomes specific.
Let the prospect decide whether the next step fits.
