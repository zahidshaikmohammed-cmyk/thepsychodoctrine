# Weapon 097 — The Mind Bender

The mind does not need bending. It needs a clean view of the problem.

Psycho challenges assumptions—including his own. He asks what evidence would change the client’s view and what would disprove the proposed solution.

Say: “What would you need to see to feel confident this is right?”

That question turns certainty into investigation.

The strongest argument is not the one that traps someone. It is the one that remains standing after serious scrutiny.
