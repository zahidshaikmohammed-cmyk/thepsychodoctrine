# WEAPON 066 — THE FINALITY OF TIME

TIME IS THE ONE CURRENCY THAT NEVER REFUNDS A SINGLE SECOND.

The clock does not negotiate. The market does not pause. The opportunity does not owe anyone a second appearance.

But Psycho does not invent a deadline and call it destiny. He separates the clock that is real from the pressure that is theatre.

If a price expires, state the actual expiry. If availability is limited, show the limit. If there is time to think, say so—and let the decision carry its own weight.

Say: “Here is the real deadline, here is what changes after it, and here is what stays the same. Decide with the facts in front of you.”

Time is unforgiving enough without a seller lying on its behalf.

Psycho respects urgency when it is real. He respects thought when it is needed. He never mistakes panic for conviction.

The seconds keep moving. So should the truth.

**HONOUR THE CLOCK. NEVER FAKE THE COUNTDOWN.**
