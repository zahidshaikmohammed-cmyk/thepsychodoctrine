# WEAPON 149 — THE FREQUENCY COMMAND

Set your own energy before the call: prepared, calm, and attentive.
Match the seriousness of the topic without mimicking or overpowering the prospect.
Use clear words, steady pacing, and accurate claims.
Watch for signs that you are talking past them.
Ask what they need rather than demanding that they feel something.
Influence should come from relevance and trust.
Your voice carries information before it carries intensity.
Transmit clarity—not control.
