# WEAPON 034 — THE ART OF DOMINANCE

## The Evolved Doctrine

**Dominate the process. Never dominate the person.**

Leadership in a sales conversation means structure: set an agenda, ask useful questions, explain options, and keep the facts clear. It does not mean claiming authority over someone else’s decision.

Try:

> “I’ll walk through the options, the trade-offs, and my recommendation. Then you can decide what fits.”

A confident recommendation is welcome when it is grounded in evidence and open to challenge. Invite questions. Admit what you do not know. Change your view when the facts require it.

The Art of Dominance is composure under scrutiny—not a performance of control.

**Lead with clarity. Stand behind the evidence. Leave the choice with the client.**

*Real authority can withstand a question—and can accept a no.*
