# WEAPON 018 — THE ENERGY FLIP

## The evolved doctrine

**Meet the energy. Regulate your own. Never hijack theirs.**

Some clients arrive cold. Some arrive excited. Some are irritated before the first sentence because they have heard too many pitches. The mistake is to treat every mood as a lock that needs picking.

The Psycho notices the temperature without pretending to own the thermostat.

If they are rushed, be concise. If they are sceptical, offer evidence. If they are excited, slow down enough to examine risk. If they are angry, don’t mirror the anger—acknowledge it and ask whether continuing is useful.

“I hear the concern. Would it help if I addressed that directly, or would you rather stop here?”

That is an energy shift built on respect. No bait-and-switch. No emotional ambush. No treating “not interested” as a puzzle to defeat.

A conversation can change when the seller stops reacting and starts listening. Calm can steady excitement; clarity can soften confusion; accountability can rebuild a little trust.

**You are not an emotional inverter. You are the steady hand on your own controls.**

The Psycho doesn’t flip the client. He changes the quality of the conversation.
