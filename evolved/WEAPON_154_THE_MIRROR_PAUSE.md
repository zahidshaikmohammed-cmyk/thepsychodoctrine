# WEAPON 154 — THE MIRROR PAUSE

Pause and reflect the prospect’s own words accurately.
Say: “You mentioned that consistency is the hardest part. Did I understand that correctly?”
Give them the chance to correct you.
Do not turn a reflection into a judgment of their character.
A mirror is useful only when it shows what is there—not what the seller wants to see.
Use the pause to listen, not corner.
Then explore what support would genuinely help.
Clarity begins with being understood.
