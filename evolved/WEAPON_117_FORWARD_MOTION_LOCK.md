# Weapon 117 — Forward Motion Lock

Forward motion becomes reliable when the next step is clear, useful, and owned by someone.

Psycho ends ambiguity by confirming the action, the person responsible, and the expected timing.

Say: “What exactly happens next, and who is handling it?”

If the client is not ready, record what remains open instead of pretending the process has advanced.

Progress is not a feeling. It is a clear step completed.
