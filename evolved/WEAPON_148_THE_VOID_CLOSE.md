# WEAPON 148 — THE VOID CLOSE

After presenting the facts, allow genuine space for a response.
Ask: “Would you like to proceed, think it over, or leave it here?”
Do not use silence as a trap or withhold guidance to provoke anxiety.
Answer questions when they arise.
If they need time, agree on whether a follow-up is welcome.
The pause belongs to the prospect too.
A close is an invitation to choose.
Let the answer be freely given.
