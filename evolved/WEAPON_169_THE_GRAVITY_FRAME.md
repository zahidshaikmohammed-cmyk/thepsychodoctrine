# WEAPON 169 — THE GRAVITY FRAME

A serious decision deserves a steady frame.
State the known facts, the material risks, and what remains uncertain.
Use measured language instead of trying to make the prospect feel psychological weight.
Ask which part deserves closer examination.
Do not describe a choice as irreversible unless it truly is.
Gravity comes from consequences that can be explained.
Calm precision invites scrutiny.
Let evidence carry the weight.
