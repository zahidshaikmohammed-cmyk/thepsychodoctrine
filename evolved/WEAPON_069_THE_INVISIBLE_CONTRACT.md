# WEAPON 069 — THE INVISIBLE CONTRACT

THERE IS NO INVISIBLE CONTRACT. THERE IS ONLY THE AGREEMENT PEOPLE ACTUALLY MAKE.

Every conversation creates expectations. A promise spoken too casually can grow teeth. A vague commitment can become tomorrow’s argument. Psycho knows words have consequences, so he makes the terms visible before anyone mistakes interest for agreement.

A conversation is not consent. A meeting is not a purchase. A question is not a signature.

Say: “Let me summarize what we’re agreeing to: the scope, the price, the responsibilities, and the next step. Is that accurate?”

Put the details in writing. Make the limits plain. Give the client space to ask questions before committing.

Psycho does not rely on hidden psychological traps or pretend a decision has already happened beneath someone’s awareness. He builds an agreement both sides can explain in daylight.

The strongest contract is not invisible. It is understood.

**NO HIDDEN TERMS. NO ASSUMED YES. MAKE THE AGREEMENT EXPLICIT.**
