# WEAPON 147 — THE TRUTH SLAB

A powerful question can clarify priorities without invoking catastrophe.
Ask: “Is this worth solving now, or is something else more important?”
Accept the answer and explore its reasoning.
Do not use mortality, illness, or imagined disaster to force a purchase.
Make the consequences concrete and proportionate to the decision.
Truth has weight without theatrics.
A useful next step should match the real stakes.
Let perspective sharpen the choice, not distort it.
