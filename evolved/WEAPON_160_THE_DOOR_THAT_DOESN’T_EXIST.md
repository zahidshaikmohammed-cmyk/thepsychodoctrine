# WEAPON 160 — THE DOOR THAT DOESN’T EXIST

Never pretend a prospect has no alternative.
Show the genuine options: proceed, wait, compare, or decline.
Explain what each option may mean, including the limits of what is known.
Ask which path best fits their goals and circumstances.
Removing choice may produce compliance, but it destroys trust.
A strong offer can stand beside alternatives.
Keep every door visible.
The right decision is the one they can defend freely.
