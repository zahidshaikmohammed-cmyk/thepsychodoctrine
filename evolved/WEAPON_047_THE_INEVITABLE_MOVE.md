Every sale is about movement.
Say: “Once you decide, you will never go back.”
The decision is not just about what they’re buying.
It’s about them taking action that can never be undone.
Psycho doesn’t ask them to think.
He makes them act — and in that act, their future is set.

## THE ACTION TRIGGER

A decision that never moves becomes another thought buried under a pile of unfinished plans.

Psycho cuts through the fog and identifies the next real action. Not ten steps. Not a speech about destiny. One move.

Sign the agreed document. Schedule the installation. Start the trial. Send the required details. Confirm the delivery date. Whatever the offer actually requires, make the action concrete and visible.

“What is the next step, and are we ready to take it?”

That is the trigger. No flailing. No frantic push. No turning a buyer’s agreement into permission to rush past the terms. A real yes must connect to a real action, and the action must match what was agreed.

Psycho does not worship motion for its own sake. A bad move is still a bad move, even when it is fast. He wants movement with direction: clear objective, clear responsibility, clear next step.

The weak seller leaves the close floating in the air. Psycho puts boots on it. The decision becomes a date, a task, a confirmation, a first step that can actually be completed.

If the buyer is not ready, find out what remains unresolved. If the decision is made, stop circling and execute.

**Weapon 047 converts decision into movement: one next step, named clearly, carried out deliberately.**
