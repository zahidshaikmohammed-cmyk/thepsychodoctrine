# Weapon 124 — Action Preference Bias

People often prefer doing something over sitting with uncertainty. Psycho recognizes that impulse and gives it a useful direction.

Before recommending action, ask whether the action will improve understanding or merely create motion.

Say: “What would this step help us learn?”

Choose the action that produces the clearest information for the least unnecessary commitment.

Action is valuable when it serves the decision. Motion alone is not progress.
