# Weapon 087 — The Beyond-Time Close

Some decisions matter long after the call ends. That is why Psycho refuses to sell only the feeling of the moment.

Look beyond the first reaction: ongoing costs, practical constraints, risk tolerance, and the work required after purchase.

Say: “Will this still make sense when today’s excitement is gone?”

A durable decision survives tomorrow’s mood.

Psycho does not promise destiny. He helps the client choose a path whose consequences they can understand and accept.
