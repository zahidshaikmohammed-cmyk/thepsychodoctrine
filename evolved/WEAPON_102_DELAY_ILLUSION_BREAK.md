# Weapon 102 — Delay Illusion Break

Delay can feel like safety because nothing changes immediately. But doing nothing is also a choice—with its own consequences.

Psycho makes the trade-off visible without pretending every delay is dangerous.

Ask: “What does waiting protect, and what does it postpone?”

Let both sides stand in the light.

The illusion breaks when the client sees that waiting is neither automatically wise nor automatically foolish. It is an option that deserves the same scrutiny as action.
