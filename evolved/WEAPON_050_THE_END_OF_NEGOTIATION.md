## THE HARD BOUNDARY

Psycho does not confuse a boundary with a tantrum.

Some terms are fixed. Some are negotiable. Some requests would destroy the value of the offer. The seller’s job is to know the difference before the conversation begins.

Know the floor. Know the limits. Know what can change and what cannot. If the price is fixed, say it plainly. If a different package exists, explain it. If the requested terms make the deal impossible, stand firm without dressing the answer in a threat.

“These are the terms I can offer. If they work for you, we can move forward. If they don’t, I understand.”

That is a hard boundary. No wobble. No fake ultimatum. No pretending there is only one option when alternatives actually exist. Psycho does not have to surrender every term to keep a buyer interested—and he does not have to trap a buyer to protect his position.

A weak seller negotiates against himself before the buyer has even answered. He discounts from fear, changes the offer without reason, and destroys his own credibility. Psycho knows the terms before he opens his mouth.

Negotiation is not automatically weakness. Unprepared negotiation is weakness. A clear boundary, honestly stated and consistently held, is force.

**Weapon 050 ends the chaos of endless bargaining: know the limit, state the terms, and stand behind the offer.**
