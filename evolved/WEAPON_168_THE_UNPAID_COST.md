# WEAPON 168 — THE UNPAID COST

Help the prospect compare the real cost of the problem with the real cost of the solution.
Include money, time, effort, risk, and the possibility of doing nothing.
Ask which trade-offs matter most to them.
Avoid inventing losses or claiming that every delay has a financial price.
Use numbers only when they are supportable.
Value is a comparison, not a threat.
Make the calculation transparent.
Let the prospect judge whether the exchange is worthwhile.
