# WEAPON 140 — THE UNCOMFORTABLE YES

Important choices can feel difficult, but discomfort is not proof that a choice is right.
Ask what is causing the unease.
Separate normal uncertainty from unresolved risk or affordability concerns.
Answer what you can and acknowledge what you cannot guarantee.
Give the prospect permission to say no or take time.
A yes should come from fit and understanding, not fear.
Confidence grows when concerns are taken seriously.
The right decision can survive a calm review.
