# WEAPON 126 — THE ECHO MOMENT

A strong line should stay with the prospect after the call—not as guilt, but as clarity.
Name the real tension in plain language.
“Sometimes the hardest part isn’t choosing. It’s admitting what matters.”
Say it once. Let the thought breathe.
Do not manufacture shame or pretend to know their private fears.
Make the point memorable because it is accurate.
Then return to the offer, its evidence, and its limits.
An echo built on truth earns a second conversation.
