# WEAPON 074 — THE MIND GAME

WORDS ARE THE SURFACE. UNDERNEATH THEM LIVES THE REAL QUESTION.

What does the client want? What do they doubt? What have they not said because the conversation has not made room for it?

Psycho listens for the gap between the stated objection and the unanswered question. He does not call silence agreement. He does not declare a decision made before the client makes it.

Say: “What part still doesn’t fit? Let’s deal with that directly.”

Then listen without preparing a counterattack.

A sharp mind can challenge a weak argument without attacking the person who made it. It can expose contradictions, test assumptions, and change its own position when the facts demand it.

That is the game: not bending someone’s mind to your will, but keeping the conversation clear when confusion tries to take control.

Psycho’s strongest advantage is not a trick hidden inside a sentence. It is the discipline to see what is actually happening.

**READ THE SIGNAL. TEST THE ASSUMPTION. SPEAK TO THE REAL ISSUE.**
