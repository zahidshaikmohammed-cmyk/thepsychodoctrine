# WEAPON 011 — THE EGO THREAD

## The evolved doctrine

**Respect the person. Challenge the assumption. Never sell to someone’s pride.**

Every buyer brings a self-image into the conversation. They want to be careful, capable, informed. Treat that identity as something to understand—not a lever to pull.

The amateur flatters the ego and hopes it buys.

The Psycho asks a sharper question: **what does this person need to believe before the facts can be judged clearly?**

Do not tell them they never miss a move. Nobody has that record. Do not turn a purchase into a test of intelligence or courage. That is not alignment; it is a trap dressed as praise.

Instead, make room for an honest answer:

“What matters most to you here—protecting capital, understanding the opportunity, or deciding whether the risk fits?”

Now the conversation has a real target. Not their pride. Their criteria.

If the evidence supports the offer, show it. If it does not, say so. A strong buyer can disagree without being made to feel small.

**The ego thread is thin. Don’t pull it until it snaps. Cut through the noise around it.**

The Psycho doesn’t need a client to feel brilliant. He needs the client to see clearly—and choose with open eyes.
