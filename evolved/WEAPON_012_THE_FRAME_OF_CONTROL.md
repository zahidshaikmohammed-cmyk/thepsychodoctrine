# WEAPON 012 — THE FRAME OF CONTROL

## The evolved doctrine

**Control the structure. Never take control of the person.**

A conversation without a frame becomes fog: scattered claims, repeated objections, details with no destination. The seller who fears silence fills it. The seller who fears rejection keeps talking.

The Psycho does neither.

Set a clean frame at the beginning:

“Let’s use a few minutes to understand your goal, review what this offer actually does, and decide whether it fits. If it doesn’t, we’ll say so.”

That is command without theatre. The purpose is clear. The path is visible. The client knows what the conversation is for—and remains free to leave it.

When sarcasm appears, stay exact. When silence appears, don’t punish it. When the buyer changes direction, find out why before dragging them back to your script.

Statements can create clarity. Questions can reveal truth. Use both with intent.

Never confuse confidence with refusing to listen. Never confuse leadership with making refusal difficult.

**A frame is not a cage. It is a map.**

The Psycho doesn’t dominate the room by shrinking everyone in it. He gives the conversation enough structure for a real decision to stand.
