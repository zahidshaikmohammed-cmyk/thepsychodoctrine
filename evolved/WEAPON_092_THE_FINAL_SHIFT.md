# Weapon 092 — The Final Shift

A final shift begins when the client sees the problem differently—not when the seller overwhelms them.

Psycho connects the original need to the available evidence, then checks whether the new perspective holds.

Say: “Has this changed how you see the options, or is something still missing?”

Let the answer shape the next move.

A shift that survives questions can become progress. A shift produced by pressure disappears when the pressure lifts.

Psycho seeks understanding that lasts beyond the call.
