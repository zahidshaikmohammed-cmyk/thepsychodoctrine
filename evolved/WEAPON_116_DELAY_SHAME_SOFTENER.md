# Weapon 116 — Delay Shame Softener

People delay for many reasons: missing information, limited money, competing priorities, or simple uncertainty.

Psycho does not turn hesitation into a character flaw. He finds the reason and responds to it directly.

Say: “It’s fine to take time. What would help you make a clearer decision?”

If the offer is right, clarity will strengthen it. If it is wrong, more pressure will not repair the fit.

Respect lowers defensiveness and makes honest answers possible.
