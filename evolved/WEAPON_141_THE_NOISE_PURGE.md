# WEAPON 141 — THE NOISE PURGE

Strip away jargon, filler, and claims that do not help the decision.
State the offer in one clear sentence.
Explain the main benefit, the main limitation, and the cost.
Ask what remains unclear.
Do not mistake confusion for agreement.
Short language should reveal the truth, not hide it.
Keep only what the prospect needs to evaluate the offer.
Minimal words. Maximum clarity.
