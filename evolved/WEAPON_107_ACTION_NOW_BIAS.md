# Weapon 107 — Action Now Bias

Action can replace uncertainty with movement, but movement alone does not prove a decision is right.

Psycho uses action to learn. Offer a reversible next step when appropriate: review the terms, test the product, or compare alternatives.

Say: “Would a small trial help you decide, without committing to the full option?”

The goal is not to rush a yes. It is to make the next useful action clear, proportionate, and informed.
