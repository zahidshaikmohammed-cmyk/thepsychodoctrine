# Weapon 081 — The Infallible Decision

No human decision is infallible. Psycho’s discipline is not pretending otherwise—it is reducing avoidable mistakes.

Test the fit. Check the cost. Name the risks. Confirm the client understands what can go wrong.

Say: “Before deciding, let’s pressure-test the reasons for and against.”

Confidence that survives scrutiny is stronger than certainty that fears questions.

Once the client decides, respect the decision. If new facts appear, revisit them without ego.

The infallible move is not never being wrong. It is refusing to hide from reality.
