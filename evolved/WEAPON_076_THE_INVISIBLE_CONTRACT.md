# Weapon 076 — The Invisible Contract

A conversation is not a contract. Attention is not consent. Psycho knows the difference—and that difference is where trust begins.

The invisible contract is the promise beneath every serious conversation: speak plainly, listen carefully, and never hide the terms.

Say: “Let’s make the decision clear before we make it official.”

No tricks. No imagined yes. No turning a first conversation into a commitment.

Psycho earns the signature by making every line visible. The strongest agreement is the one the client understands—and chooses freely.
