# WEAPON 161 — THE HOPE DETONATOR

Hope becomes useful when it is paired with a testable next step.
Ask: “What would turn this goal from an idea into a plan?”
Choose one action that is practical, measurable, and within the prospect’s control.
Do not mock hope or promise that action guarantees success.
Set a way to review progress and adjust if the evidence changes.
Motivation can start movement; a plan sustains it.
Detonate vagueness, not the person.
Let reality give hope its structure.
