# Weapon 084 — The Decision of the Heart

Emotion opens the door. Evidence decides whether the room is safe.

Psycho hears what matters to the client: security, ambition, independence, or peace of mind. He connects those motives to the offer without promising a fantasy.

Say: “What outcome matters most—and what would make this feel like the wrong choice?”

The heart deserves honesty as much as the mind deserves facts.

A decision with feeling and understanding can stand. A decision built only on excitement may collapse when the excitement fades.
