# WEAPON 158 — THE COMMAND WITHOUT SOUND

After explaining the offer, stop talking long enough for a real answer.
Say: “I’ve covered the key points. What questions remain?”
Do not interpret silence as agreement or use it to make someone uncomfortable.
Wait patiently, then answer what they ask.
If they are ready, confirm their choice clearly.
If not, respect that too.
Silence is space for thought—not a hidden command.
The next word belongs to the prospect.
