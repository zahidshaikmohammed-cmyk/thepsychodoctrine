# Weapon 110 — Opportunity Decay Signal

Opportunities can change over time, but the change must be real and verifiable.

Psycho identifies the facts, the unknowns, and the practical next step.

Say: “Here is what may change if we wait, and here is what we cannot know yet.”

Do not invent a loss or promise an outcome. A useful signal improves understanding; it does not claim the future is already decided.
