# WEAPON 030 — THE YES SPARK

## The Evolved Doctrine

**A real yes is informed—not surrendered.**

Listen to the quality of the agreement, not just the sound of the word. A rushed yes can hide confusion. A quiet yes can be thoughtful. Confirm that the client understands the terms, the risks, and the next step.

Ask:

> “Before we proceed, do you feel clear on the benefits, the risks, and what happens next?”

Then give them room to answer honestly. If something is unclear, explain it. If they need time, respect it. If they choose not to proceed, accept the decision professionally.

No victory lap. No pressure after consent. No treating payment as proof that every concern has vanished.

The Yes Spark is not the moment someone gives in. It is the moment a clear decision becomes a deliberate action.

**Earn the yes. Confirm the understanding. Honour the choice.**

*The strongest close is one the client can stand behind tomorrow.*
