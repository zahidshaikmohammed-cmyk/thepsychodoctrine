# Weapon 101 — Later Collapse Trigger

“Later” can be a plan—or a hiding place.

Psycho does not attack the pause. He examines it. What will waiting clarify? What will it delay? What could change while the decision sits untouched?

Ask: “What do you expect to know later that we cannot establish today?”

If there is a real answer, gather it. If there is none, name the loop without shame.

The trigger is not fear of tomorrow. It is the moment a vague delay becomes a clear next step.
