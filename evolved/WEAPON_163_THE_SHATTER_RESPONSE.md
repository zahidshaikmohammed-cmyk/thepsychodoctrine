# WEAPON 163 — THE SHATTER RESPONSE

When someone says they are not ready, treat it as information—not a weakness to break.
Ask: “What would need to change for you to feel ready?”
They may need facts, time, affordability, or a different solution.
Address the concern if you can; accept it if you cannot.
Do not diagnose a wound or insist they were born ready.
A strong response makes room for an honest no.
Break confusion with clarity, not the person’s confidence.
That is disciplined objection handling.
