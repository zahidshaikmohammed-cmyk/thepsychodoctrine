# WEAPON 133 — THE NO RETURN ZONE

Truth can change a conversation without trapping anyone in it.
Summarize the facts the prospect has acknowledged.
Ask what conclusion they draw from those facts.
Do not frame a past statement as a binding commitment.
People may reconsider when new information appears.
Keep the way forward open, including the option to decline.
A decision is stronger when it can withstand review.
No bridge needs to be burned to move ahead.
