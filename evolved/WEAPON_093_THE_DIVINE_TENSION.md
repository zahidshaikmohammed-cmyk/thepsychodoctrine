# Weapon 093 — The Divine Tension

Tension can signal that a decision matters. It can also signal that something is unclear.

Psycho does not automatically intensify it. He diagnoses it.

Is the concern price, risk, timing, trust, or fit? Name the source before trying to solve it.

Say: “What is creating the hesitation?”

The question is direct. The answer is allowed to be honest.

Tension becomes useful when it points toward the truth. It becomes destructive when the seller treats discomfort as a lever.
