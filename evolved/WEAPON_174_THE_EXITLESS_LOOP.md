# WEAPON 174 — THE EXITLESS LOOP

Help the prospect notice a repeated decision pattern without claiming they are trapped.
Ask: “What usually happens between considering a change and taking the next step?”
Listen for the practical point where progress stalls.
Choose one small experiment that can be reviewed later.
Do not insist that buying is the only way to make progress.
Other options may fit better.
A pattern can be changed through understanding and practice.
Create a route forward, not an exitless loop.
