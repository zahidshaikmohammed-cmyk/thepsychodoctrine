# WEAPON 171 — THE CUBICLE WAR ENTRY

If routine feels limiting, explore what the prospect wants to change before proposing an exit.
Ask what they value about their current work and what they would like to improve.
Do not call a job captivity or promise that a product will free them.
Compare realistic alternatives, including the cost and uncertainty of change.
Ambition deserves a plan, not contempt for the present.
Make the next move practical.
Build a bridge before selling the destination.
