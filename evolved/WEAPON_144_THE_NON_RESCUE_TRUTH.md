# WEAPON 144 — THE NON RESCUE TRUTH

You cannot rescue a prospect from every problem, and you should not claim to.
Explain what the product can help with—and what remains outside its scope.
Ask what outcome they expect and correct unrealistic assumptions.
If the offer is a poor fit, say so.
Truth may disappoint in the moment, but it prevents a worse surprise later.
Your role is to inform and guide, not to take over their life.
Reveal the limits as clearly as the benefits.
