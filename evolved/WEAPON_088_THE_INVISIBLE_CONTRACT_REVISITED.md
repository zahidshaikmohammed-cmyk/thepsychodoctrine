# Weapon 088 — The Invisible Contract — Revisited

An agreement is real only when both sides know what they are agreeing to.

Psycho makes the invisible visible: responsibilities, timelines, fees, limitations, and what happens if circumstances change.

Say: “Let’s put every important expectation into plain language.”

No implied consent. No buried condition. No surprise after the signature.

Trust is not a mysterious force binding people together. It is a pattern of clear promises kept over time.

Make the terms visible, and let the client choose.
