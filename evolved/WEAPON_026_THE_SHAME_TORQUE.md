# WEAPON 026 — THE SHAME TORQUE

## The Evolved Doctrine

**Accountability can sting. Humiliation is not a strategy.**

When someone delays, do not assume cowardice. They may be protecting their money, checking the facts, or recognizing a risk you have not addressed.

Bring the pattern into the light without attacking the person.

> “Last time, what made the decision difficult—and what would you do differently with what you know now?”

Let them examine their own history. Do not weaponize regret, compare them with other buyers, or imply that a cautious decision makes them weak.

The point is not to make the past hurt enough that they buy. It is to help them decide whether the present opportunity fits their goals and risk tolerance.

**Challenge the pattern. Protect the person.**

*Real strength can face regret without selling someone a cure for it.*
