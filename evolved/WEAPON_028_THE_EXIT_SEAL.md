# WEAPON 028 — THE EXIT SEAL

## The Evolved Doctrine

**A close is a clear next step—not a locked door.**

When the client decides, confirm exactly what they agreed to, what happens next, and any terms or risks that still matter. Never imply that paperwork is irreversible before it actually is.

Try:

> “Would you like me to proceed with the next step, or is there anything you want to review first?”

If they say yes, document the agreement and follow the proper process. If they hesitate or change their mind, clarify without punishment.

Closure should reduce confusion, not remove the chance to think. A real commitment can survive a clear explanation.

The Exit Seal closes the loop: terms understood, next step confirmed, expectations aligned.

**No assumed consent. No false finality.**

*Seal the process—not the person’s options.*
