# WEAPON 022 — THE TIME RAZOR

## The Evolved Doctrine

**Time matters. Fake countdowns are noise.**

Markets move. Offers can expire. Conditions can change. But urgency is only useful when it is real, specific, and verifiable.

State the actual deadline. Explain what may change and what is still unknown. If there is no deadline, do not manufacture one.

> “Here is the current window, here is what could change, and here is what we cannot know in advance.”

Show the cost of waiting as a scenario—not a prophecy. A missed opportunity is possible; so is avoiding a bad decision.

The Time Razor cuts through vagueness, not through a client’s right to think.

**No invented scarcity. No guaranteed outcome. No clock built from fear.**

*Time is a factor. Truth is the blade.*
