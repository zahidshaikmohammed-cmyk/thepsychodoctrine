# WEAPON 063 — THE DECISION IS A TRUTH

A DECISION IS NOT MAGIC. IT IS THE MOMENT THE FACTS BECOME CLEAR ENOUGH TO ACT ON.

The Psycho cuts through the fog: what is needed, what is offered, what it costs, and what remains uncertain.

“This is what the solution can do. This is what it cannot. Does it meet the need?”

That is the blade. Not a claim to know another person’s soul. Not a trap built from false certainty. A truth that can be examined without collapsing.

The offer must earn its place. The evidence must support the promise. The client must be free to say yes, no, or not yet.

Psycho does not erase options to make one answer look inevitable. He makes the real differences visible until the choice can be made with open eyes.

A truth that needs intimidation is not truth. A decision that cannot survive questions is not ready.

**MAKE THE FACTS CLEAR. LET THE DECISION BELONG TO THE CLIENT.**
