# WEAPON 071 — THE FINAL PUSH

THE FINAL PUSH IS NOT A SHOVE. IT IS THE LAST CLEAN QUESTION BEFORE ACTION.

The case has been made. The goal is clear. The terms are visible. The objections have been faced without smoke or theatre.

Now Psycho brings the conversation to its point.

“You’ve seen the offer, the cost, and the trade-offs. Are you ready to move forward—or is something still unresolved?”

Then silence.

No invented yes. No pretending hesitation means consent. No pushing harder just because the room has gone quiet.

If the answer is yes, lock in the next step and execute. If a question remains, answer it. If the fit is wrong, let the deal die standing.

Psycho’s final push is precision: one clear question, one honest answer, one defined next move.

The weak seller keeps pushing because he fears the verdict. Psycho can face it.

**ASK CLEAN. HEAR THE ANSWER. MOVE WITH PURPOSE.**
