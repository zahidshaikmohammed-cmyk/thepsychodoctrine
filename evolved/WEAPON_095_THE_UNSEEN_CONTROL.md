# Weapon 095 — The Unseen Control

The strongest influence in a conversation is often the structure no one notices: preparation, accurate information, calm pacing, and questions that uncover the real issue.

Psycho controls his own conduct—not another person’s mind.

Say: “Let’s separate what we know from what we still need to verify.”

That simple discipline cuts through confusion without theatrics.

The unseen force is preparation. The visible result is a conversation where facts are clear and the client remains in control of the choice.
