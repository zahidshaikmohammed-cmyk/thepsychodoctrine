# WEAPON 015 — THE TRUST RIPPER

## The evolved doctrine

**Don’t rip trust apart. Strip the conversation of what makes trust impossible.**

People have heard polished pitches before. Some have met brokers who promised certainty, hid the downside, or disappeared when the story changed. Suspicion may be earned.

Do not exploit that resentment by pretending to be the one honest voice in a corrupt world. Do not invent failed-trade stories or borrow someone else’s pain to sound battle-tested.

Start with candour:

“You may have heard promises before. I won’t ask you to trust a promise. I’ll show you what can be verified, what remains uncertain, and where the risks sit.”

Then make the evidence inspectable. Explain fees. Name limitations. Distinguish historical performance from future outcomes. If a claim cannot be substantiated, remove it.

Trust is not created by sounding rough, confessing a convenient flaw, or attacking every other seller. It is earned when the facts remain consistent after the pitch is over.

And if the client is not convinced, don’t punish the doubt. Find the missing evidence—or accept that confidence has not yet been earned.

**No mask to tear. No enemy to invent. No promise that outruns the proof.**

The Psycho doesn’t break trust to enter. He removes the fog so trust, if deserved, has somewhere solid to stand.
