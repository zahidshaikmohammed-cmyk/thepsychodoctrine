# WEAPON 073 — THE PRESSURE OF DECISION

EVERY DECISION CARRIES WEIGHT. INDECISION CARRIES A COST TOO.

Time passes whether a choice is made or not. Problems can remain unsolved. Opportunities can change. Resources can be spent elsewhere. Those consequences deserve daylight—not exaggeration.

Psycho puts the real cost beside the real commitment.

Say: “What is waiting costing you, and what would moving forward actually require?”

Compare both sides honestly: the cost of acting, the cost of waiting, the risk of being wrong, and the cost of doing nothing. No invented catastrophe. No claim that one purchase decides a person’s entire destiny.

Pressure is useful when it comes from reality and points toward a clear choice. It becomes manipulation when the seller fabricates the stakes.

Psycho does not erase uncertainty. He puts it on the table, where it can be examined instead of feared.

**MEASURE THE COST. NAME THE RISK. DECIDE WITH OPEN EYES.**
