# WEAPON 156 — THE EYE OF DECISION

When a decision feels chaotic, help the prospect separate signal from noise.
Summarize the options and the known facts.
Name the key uncertainty instead of pretending it does not exist.
Ask which factor deserves the most attention.
Do not suggest that acting immediately controls every outcome.
Stillness is useful when it creates room to think.
Keep the risks visible and the next step proportionate.
Be the calm inside the storm—not the storm itself.
