# WEAPON 153 — THE SELF DEATH SCRIPT

Change can mean leaving an old habit behind; it does not require rejecting the self.
Ask what behaviour the prospect wants to change and why.
Make the first step concrete and achievable.
Describe the product as a tool, not a rebirth guarantee.
Avoid language that shames the person they have been.
Progress is built through repeated choices, not one dramatic purchase.
Let the past inform the plan without defining the person.
Evolution is construction, not erasure.
