# WEAPON 067 — THE MIND GAME

THE FIRST BATTLE IS NOT AGAINST THE CLIENT. IT IS AGAINST THE NOISE IN THE CONVERSATION.

Selling is a mind game—but not a licence to bend another mind to someone else’s will.

Psycho listens for the signal beneath the words. The goal. The fear. The unanswered question. The gap between what the client wants and what the offer can actually deliver.

Say: “What still doesn’t line up for you? Let’s put it into words and deal with it directly.”

Then the real work begins. No mind-reading. No pretending the decision has already been made. No declaring resistance defeated because the client went quiet.

The strongest mind in the room is the one that can hold two truths at once: confidence in the offer and willingness to discover it may not fit.

Psycho doesn’t bend reality until the client agrees. He sharpens the conversation until the facts can be seen.

**MASTER THE CONVERSATION. NEVER CLAIM OWNERSHIP OF ANOTHER MIND.**
