## PSYCHO EVOLUTION — REVEAL, DON’T PROJECT

THE MOST POWERFUL MOMENT IS WHEN THE CLIENT RECOGNIZES THEIR OWN REALITY.

Psycho doesn’t pretend to know a stranger’s hidden truth. He asks precise questions, listens for the pattern, and reflects back what the client has actually said.

What are they trying to change? What have they already tried? What keeps getting in the way? What would success look like in measurable terms?

**Say:** “Let me check that I’ve understood. The real issue is this, the outcome you want is that, and the obstacle is here. Have I got it right?”

That question can change the entire conversation. Not because it corners the client, but because it gives them a clean mirror.

If the reflection is wrong, correct it. If the product cannot deliver the outcome, say so. If the fit is real, connect the features to the goal with evidence.

Psycho doesn’t manufacture revelation. He creates the conditions for clear recognition—and lets the facts speak.

**THE RULE:** Ask. Listen. Reflect accurately. Never claim ownership of another person’s truth.
