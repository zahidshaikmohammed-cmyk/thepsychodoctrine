# Weapon 085 — The Crisis of Decision

A decision can feel like a crisis when the stakes are high. Psycho lowers the noise and separates the real choices.

What is known? What remains uncertain? What happens if the client waits? What happens if they proceed?

Say: “Let’s make the consequences of each option visible.”

No shame for caution. No threat disguised as motivation.

When the facts are laid out, the next step often becomes simpler. If it remains unclear, more information—not more pressure—is the answer.
