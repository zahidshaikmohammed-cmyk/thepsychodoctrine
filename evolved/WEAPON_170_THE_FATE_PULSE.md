# WEAPON 170 — THE FATE PULSE

Do not claim that a sales conversation was destined or that fate requires a purchase.
Make the moment useful by connecting it to a goal the prospect has already named.
Ask whether the timing genuinely works for them.
Explain what is available now and what could change, with evidence.
Chance meetings can matter without becoming prophecy.
The prospect owns the meaning and the decision.
Make relevance real.
Let choice—not fate—set the direction.
