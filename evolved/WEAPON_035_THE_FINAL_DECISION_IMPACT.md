# WEAPON 035 — THE FINAL DECISION IMPACT

## The Evolved Doctrine

**A decision matters because of what follows—not because you declare it irreversible.**

When a client chooses, make the next steps concrete. Confirm the terms, the responsibilities, the costs, and the risks. Explain what can be changed or cancelled, where that is applicable, and what happens after the agreement.

> “Let’s confirm what this decision means in practice, so there are no surprises later.”

Do not tell someone they have already chosen before they have. Do not promise that one purchase will transform their future. Let the value be measured by the real outcome.

A meaningful decision can change a direction. It does not erase uncertainty or remove the need for follow-through.

The Final Decision Impact is accountability after the close.

**Confirm the choice. Honour the terms. Deliver what was promised.**

*The sale ends at agreement. Your credibility is decided by what happens next.*
