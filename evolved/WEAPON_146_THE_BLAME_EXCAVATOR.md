# WEAPON 146 — THE BLAME EXCAVATOR

When a pattern keeps repeating, explore the obstacle without assigning blame.
Ask: “What has made this difficult to act on so far?”
Listen for practical barriers and constraints.
Offer one realistic adjustment, not a character judgment.
Do not speculate about family history or shame someone for inherited circumstances.
Responsibility works best when paired with agency and support.
Find the barrier that can actually be changed.
Then agree on a useful next step—or stop.
