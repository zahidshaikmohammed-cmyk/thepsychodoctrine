# Weapon 120 — Clock Compression Effect

A short conversation can still be useful when it is structured well.

Psycho respects time by focusing on the client’s goal, the most important question, and the next useful step.

Say: “We have ten minutes. Which question would be most valuable to answer first?”

Do not confuse a tight agenda with a rushed decision. If the matter needs more time, schedule it honestly.

Compression should remove waste, never remove understanding.
