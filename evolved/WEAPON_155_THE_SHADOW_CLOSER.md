# WEAPON 155 — THE SHADOW CLOSER

Personal experience can give a message weight when it is real and relevant.
Share a brief lesson without presenting your story as proof that everyone will succeed.
Explain what you learned, what changed, and what remains uncertain.
Never invent trauma or use scars as leverage.
A prospect needs honest context, not a performance of pain.
Let experience illuminate the path while evidence evaluates the offer.
Credibility comes from candour.
Purpose does not require vengeance.
