# Weapon 079 — The Urgency of Now

Now has power—but only when the reason is real.

A market window may move. An offer may have a verified deadline. A client may simply need more time. Psycho separates facts from theatre.

Say: “Here is what is time-sensitive, here is what is not, and here is what waiting changes.”

No invented countdown. No prophecy dressed as certainty.

Urgency is useful when it informs. It becomes weakness when it deceives.

Psycho moves decisively because the facts are ready—not because the client has been cornered.
